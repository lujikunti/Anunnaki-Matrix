# ANK Geography Layer v1

This is the canonical shared geography contract for Michael Noah and The General Counsel.

## What it is
A neutral registry of stable place IDs and navigation regions. It exists so MN and GC do not maintain competing country metadata.

## What it is not
It is not a country encyclopaedia, a legal database, a curriculum catalogue, a political-status engine or a source of substantive country copy.

## Product overlays
Each product owns its own bindings:
- **Michael Noah** binds a place/region to existing learning, language, story, curriculum or library objects.
- **The General Counsel** binds a jurisdiction/region to existing legal, transaction, construction, procurement or cross-border objects.

Registry presence never means content is available. Product overlays must assign one of:
- AVAILABLE — substantive usable content exists;
- GROWING — some real material exists, but the collection is incomplete;
- PLANNED — no substantive public collection exists.

## Geography rules
- language is many-to-many;
- regional groupings are navigation aids, not legal or political conclusions;
- disputed or complex territorial questions are handled neutrally and at source level;
- no country page is generated merely because a registry record exists;
- no fake completion percentage;
- no flags required for primary navigation;
- broad international regions may be empty until demand justifies expansion.

## Africa basis
The v1 Africa place set mirrors the 55-jurisdiction catalogue already governed by the GC Doing Business in Africa system. ANK subregions are discovery groupings only.

## International basis
International regions exist as empty-capable navigation nodes. The United Kingdom is the only v1 international seed because the requested cross-border architecture explicitly uses Kenya → United Kingdom as an example. This is not a claim that GC or MN already has a UK collection.

## Update rule
Change this registry first. Product repositories may carry versioned snapshots for deterministic builds, but those snapshots must preserve this registry version and fingerprint and must not independently edit place metadata.
