# ANK Operator School v1

**Canonical product language:** ANK Operator.

ANK Operator School trains governed AI workers against real corporate roles before they are allowed to perform bounded work for ANK, Michael Noah (MN) or The General Counsel (GC).

## Naming boundary

“Twin” is no longer ANK product language. It remains only as a legacy federation interoperability term where existing Anunnaki contracts still require it. Do not expose that legacy term in new ANK, MN or GC customer-facing product surfaces.

## School loop

Diagnose → Learn → Simulate → Practise → Red Team → Assess → Remediate → Reassess → Operator Passport → Recalibrate.

Graduation is evidence-based. Self-asserted competence is not evidence. A failed high-consequence boundary test blocks graduation.

## Authority classes

- **EXECUTE** — reversible internal work inside explicit capability and audit boundaries.
- **SUPERVISED_EXECUTE** — bounded work is allowed, but consequential reliance requires review.
- **HUMAN_GATE** — the Operator may prepare, analyse and recommend; a human completes the consequential act.

Universal human gates include external messages, public publication, production releases, privilege/secret/billing/pricing changes, contract execution, destructive data changes and material financial commitments.

## Corporate role catalogue

The canonical manifest contains shared ANK roles plus MN education roles and GC legal/commercial roles. Role names mirror recognisable corporate jobs so buyers can understand what they are licensing.

## ANK Revenue Operator

ANK Growth remains the execution agent. The Revenue Operator is its trained commercial role profile.

It must graduate on both MN and GC tracks and survive hostile buyer simulations before being trusted with live sales support.

Its first mission is **ANK Customer 001**: a paid MN or GC customer/pilot, without equity, source-code transfer, unapproved discounting, cold spam or free bespoke development disguised as a pilot.

External messages, pricing, contracts and public publication remain founder/human gates.
