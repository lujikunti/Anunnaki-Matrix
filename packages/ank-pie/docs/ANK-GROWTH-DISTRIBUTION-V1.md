# ANK Growth & Distribution v1

ANK Growth is the pull-first growth function inside ANK PIE. It exists to make MN and GC discoverable without turning either product into a spam operation.

## Operating model

ANK Growth may autonomously research public market signals, identify search and distribution opportunities, draft founder-led content, repurpose approved material, map partners and communities, qualify inbound demand, analyse attribution, and propose experiments.

It may not autonomously contact people or publish to the public. `external_message` and `public_publication` remain human gates in the ANK PIE control plane.

Cold email and cold DMs are not part of the default distribution model. The agent also blocks unsolicited bulk messaging, scraped personal-contact acquisition, fake reviews, astroturfing, and impersonation.

## Pull-first distribution loop

1. Sense — use public research, ANK Observatory evidence, product telemetry and support questions to identify real demand.
2. Create — turn product capability into useful public artifacts: demos, calculators, diagnostics, RED Notes, parent guides, explainers, benchmarks, diagrams and case studies.
3. Distribute — prepare channel-specific packages for search, founder-led social, video, communities, directories, events, partners and referrals.
4. Capture — route opt-in visitors into demos, waitlists, trials, webinars, downloads or product entry points.
5. Qualify — score inbound intent without purchasing or scraping personal contact lists.
6. Learn — attribute traffic, activation, qualified pipeline, referrals and conversion to the originating asset/channel.
7. Repeat — scale only channels with evidence.

## Human gates

A human must approve any public post or publication, direct external message, customer-identifiable case study or testimonial, paid media spend or billing change, and production release.

## Success measures

Track qualified inbound demand, non-branded search discovery, demo starts, referral activation, partner-sourced traffic, content-assisted conversion, webinar/event conversion, retained paid users/customers, and cost per qualified opportunity where spend exists.

Vanity impressions are diagnostic only; they are not the north star.
