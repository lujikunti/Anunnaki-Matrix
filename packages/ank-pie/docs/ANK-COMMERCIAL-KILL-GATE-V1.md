# ANK Commercial Kill Gate v1

PIE is not a product factory. Revenue is accountable for proving that a buyer has a specific urgent problem, that the offer can be delivered truthfully, and that real customers will pay before ANK spends meaningful product or design effort.

## The rule

**SWOT earns a product the right to be tested. Paid behaviour earns it the right to be sold at scale.**

For every MN or GC offer PIE runs:

**PRODUCT TRUTH → DEMAND EVIDENCE → SWOT → COST → LEAN PAID TEST → SELL / PARK / KILL**

The default state for an unproven SKU is **PARK**.

## Status meanings

- **TEST_NOW** — built capability + credible demand evidence. PIE may make only the brochure/demo/website material required to test willingness to pay.
- **TEST_NOW_BLOCKED** — evidence is strong, but a release/payment/truth gate prevents taking money yet.
- **SELL_NOW** — paid validation has passed.
- **PARK** — do not make polished assets, expand engineering or spend on distribution.
- **KILL** — remove from active commercial planning unless new evidence materially changes the case.

## New-product discipline

PIE must not send an idea directly into engineering. A proposed new SKU must first use existing capabilities, a clearly disclosed concierge/manual pilot, or another reversible test to obtain paid-market evidence. No invented demand, invented testimonial, invented capability or invented cost is permitted.

## Costing

Revenue owns the commercial decision. Product verifies what exists. Finance/Audit verifies cost assumptions. Red Team attacks the claims and the economics.

Every cost model includes AI/API, compute/storage/bandwidth, payment processing, human/professional review, support, asset delivery, statutory/tax cost where applicable, and refund/failure contingency.

Existing public prices are not silently changed. Any price change remains a human gate.

## Asset rule

A brochure is a test instrument, not decoration. It must make the buyer understand:

1. the urgent problem;
2. exactly what the product does;
3. exactly what the buyer provides;
4. exactly what ANK delivers;
5. turnaround;
6. limitations and professional/release boundaries;
7. tested price;
8. one concrete demo;
9. the next buying action.

Deep capability brochures, demo scripts and website product pages are generated only for TEST_NOW / TEST_NOW_BLOCKED / SELL_NOW products.
