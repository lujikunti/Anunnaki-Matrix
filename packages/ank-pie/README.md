# ANK PIE

Shared governed-agent manifests for Anunnaki Matrix products.

## ANK Growth

`agents/ank-growth-v1.json` is the canonical pull-first Growth & Distribution worker. Product repositories mirror the manifest into their live ANK PIE control policies because MN and GC currently maintain separate Supabase control-plane instances.

The agent may research, draft, map channels, qualify inbound demand and analyse attribution autonomously. External messaging and public publication require human approval. Cold email and cold DMs are disabled by design.

## ANK Operator profiles

Operator profiles are a naming, packaging and training layer over ANK PIE; they are **not** another agent runtime or trust system. `operators/operator-profiles-v1.json` maps familiar corporate roles to existing governed agents. All capability trust, promotion, demotion and execution authority remain with `ANK_AGENT_APPRENTICESHIP_V1` and the existing PIE worker allowlists.

The Revenue Operator reuses `ank-growth`. Its additional commercial capabilities begin as training-only and cannot execute merely because they appear in the profile or agent manifest.

## GC Foundry training

`training/gc-foundry-v1.json` teaches existing PIE agents the GC Foundry's 18 governed capability families and six-stage workflow grammar. It is curriculum/evaluation context only: all capabilities begin SHADOW, PIE apprenticeship remains the sole trust authority, and no worker allowlist or professional/action authority is created.

Raw Foundry source text and non-admitted semantic extractions are not copied into PIE. Curated capability/workflow registries and professionally governed Lawyer School patterns are the training sources; quarantined, rights-blocked, stale/currentness-unverified and provenance-incomplete extractions stay excluded.

The natal chart is never a substitute for runtime authorization. Machine policy, PIE apprenticeship, product worker allowlists, human gates and kill switches remain authoritative.

### African-first standards rule

PIE must resolve applicable African continental, regional, national and sector sources before defaulting to foreign guidance. Africa is not treated as one jurisdiction. Binding country law and verified treaty applicability take precedence; non-binding African frameworks are first-class governance inputs; ISO/NIST/OWASP remain important complementary controls.

The registry intentionally marks the South African March 2026 draft AI policy as `WITHDRAWN_REFERENCE_ONLY`. It may be used as a currentness test case, not represented as binding or final policy. ISO and other licensed standards are metadata/control references unless ANK has lawful access for the intended full-text use.
