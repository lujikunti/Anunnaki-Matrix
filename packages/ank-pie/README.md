# ANK PIE

Shared governed-agent manifests for Anunnaki Matrix products.

## ANK Growth

`agents/ank-growth-v1.json` is the canonical pull-first Growth & Distribution worker. Product repositories mirror the manifest into their live ANK PIE control policies because MN and GC currently maintain separate Supabase control-plane instances.

The agent may research, draft, map channels, qualify inbound demand and analyse attribution autonomously. External messaging and public publication require human approval. Cold email and cold DMs are disabled by design.

## ANK Operator School

`operators/operator-school-v1.json` is the canonical governed corporate-role training catalogue for ANK, MN and GC. New product language uses **ANK Operator**. Legacy **Twin** terminology is confined to backward-compatible federation contracts until those interfaces are safely migrated.

`operators/revenue-operator-v1.json` trains the existing `ank-growth` execution agent as ANK's Revenue Operator. It begins as `APPRENTICE`, must pass MN and GC sales simulations plus adversarial red-team cases, and cannot send external messages, change pricing, sign contracts or publish without a human gate.
