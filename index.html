Anunnaki AI

From information to auditable intelligence.

Anunnaki AI is the artificial-intelligence platform being developed by Anunnaki Matrix.

It is designed to help organisations ingest and organise information, examine evidence, support structured analysis and produce outputs that can be reviewed against their underlying sources.

The platform is currently under active development. This website describes the company and its technology; it does not represent unfinished functionality as commercially available.
